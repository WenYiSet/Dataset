# Rights and reuse

The WenYi metadata describes pattern images collected from published and institutional sources. The repository license covers the metadata and software contributed by the WenYi project. It does not transfer copyright or other rights in the underlying source images or publications.

Before reproducing an image, users should consult the source reference in the metadata, identify the relevant rights holder, and comply with the source institution's or publisher's terms. Records with incomplete source information should not be treated as evidence that an image is free of restrictions.

The online Explorer provides research access to the collection. Display in the Explorer does not constitute a separate grant of reuse rights.
